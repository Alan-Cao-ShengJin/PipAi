# Historical statements and quarterly derivation

## Establish the basis

Record fiscal year-end, accounting framework, consolidated/parent scope, reporting currency/scale, source vintage and whether each column is actual or forecast. Use the issuer's own statements and notes. Vendor standardisations are comparison data, not instructions to overwrite reported classifications.

Locate periods and metrics from headers, labels and a reviewed mapping. Resolve duplicated labels explicitly. Do not assume `H44` is always net income or that one template's account list fits every issuer. Preserve source signs and document normalised model signs.

## Derive only comparable additive flows

For matching cumulative income-statement or cash-flow flows, Q2 = H1 - Q1; Q3 = 9M - H1; Q4 = FY - 9M. Check the same fiscal year, scope, currency, classification and restatement basis first. Prefer directly disclosed single-quarter figures as an additional check when available.

Do not subtract balance-sheet snapshots to obtain a quarter's balance; a difference is a movement requiring a roll-forward. Do not subtract EPS, average shares, margins, turnover ratios or effective tax rates. Use appropriately weighted share counts and recompute ratios from their underlying amounts. Negative or tiny denominators require explicit interpretation rather than a misleading growth percentage.

Quarterly figures derived from actual cumulative reports may be displayed as actuals, but their derivation and verification status stay in the evidence record. Never label a vendor reconciliation as passed if the vendor was unavailable. Compare within disclosed rounding tolerances, not fake precision beyond source units.

## Reconcile

- Quarter flow sums reconcile to cumulative/FY amounts on the same basis.
- Income-statement bridges use the issuer's classification and separately identify equity-method items, minority interests and nonrecurring adjustments.
- Assets reconcile to liabilities plus equity, with any rounding residual shown.
- Cash-flow changes reconcile to the corresponding cash/equivalent definition, with FX and reclassification differences identified.
- Debt, PPE, working capital and equity movements reconcile through explicit cash/noncash bridges where material.
- Reported EPS and share-count bridges use the issuer's basic/diluted treatment and relevant share events; buybacks/ESOP shares cannot be treated by a universal shortcut.

Convert currencies only for a stated analytical purpose and preserve native figures. Distinguish price-date FX, balance-sheet closing rates and period flow translation. Do not apply a period-end rate to all historical income and cash flows. Use the actual reporting framework and appropriate dates/averages; do not automatically restate a Hong Kong issuer into an A-share account structure.

When actuals replace a forecast quarter, preserve the previous forecast for attribution, update the actual/forecast labels and use reported FY data rather than an obsolete FY formula. Recheck downstream references after any period insertion or column movement.
