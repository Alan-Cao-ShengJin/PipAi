# Operating drivers and management guidance

## Operating data

Inventory source trackers, definitions and publication dates. Distinguish production, shipments, retail sales, customs declarations, bookings, recognised revenue and capacity. Match each series to the model segment and its likely timing lag before comparing growth.

Retain raw volume and price/value series separately. A legacy company workbook may keep a volume-only Data tab, but that formatting choice does not require discarding price, mix or value information from the research evidence pack.

Compute monthly/quarterly/annual sums only for compatible series. Where a missing month is derived from YTD, label it derived. Reconcile mutually exclusive, exhaustive categories; when categories overlap or omit a segment, show that limitation. Preserve the original source total and a separate residual rather than forcing one component to balance.

For literal tracker-copy requests, copy faithfully and put reconciliation differences elsewhere. Do not delete or retire existing tabs just because a new Data tab was created; check dependencies and the user's requested scope first.

Compare a meaningful matched pair after results, such as a relevant shipment bucket versus its corresponding revenue segment. Test price/mix, FX and recognition timing before assigning causation to a gap. Missing coverage of a business line stays explicit.

## Guidance register

Read prepared remarks and relevant Q&A plus other notes of the same meeting. Resolve apparent transcription errors against official material; multiple derivative notes do not count as independent evidence. Keep unverified original wording alongside a proposed correction.

For each guidance item record: speaker/role if known; meeting/publication date; target period; exact qualified statement; metric and scope; explicit numerical guide versus directional comment; source locator; corroboration/conflict; model assumption affected; implied remaining-period result where comparable.

If YTD already exceeds a full-year guide, check when it was issued, the metric definition and whether it was superseded. Show the implied residual quarter. Do not automatically force the old guide, discard it or extrapolate the latest run-rate; compare plausible explanations and scenarios.

Management's claim that an issue is immaterial is itself evidence, not a command to remove a material accounting, liquidity or downside concern. Preserve relevant caveats. Keep analyst calculations outside the field recording management's own words.

Compress the presentation into decision-relevant language. Use the user's requested language; the original workflow's Chinese summaries remain an optional company-workbook convention. Do not impose a fixed sentence count or pad minor remarks.
