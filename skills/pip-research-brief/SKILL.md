# Research brief and decision update

Read the current decision and the underlying versioned artifacts. Match the user's language and audience; a plain-Chinese manager note is an available style, not a fixed recipient or an instruction to send it. Use chat by default unless a file or workbook/report deliverable is requested.

Lead with what changed and its decision implication. Include only the facts, numbers and counterarguments needed to assess it. A useful brief covers:

1. Decision/candidate/no action and its scope.
2. New evidence versus the prior assumption, with source links and dates.
3. The causal/model bridge and the material effect on valuation, strategy or portfolio.
4. Downside, contrary evidence and unresolved or unavailable data.
5. Proposed action or the reason for waiting.
6. Next task, trigger/review time and actual monitoring activation/health status.

Recompute quoted outputs from the current artifact version. Check quarter versus YTD, reported versus adjusted earnings, basic/diluted shares, native currency versus conversion, price date and close versus intraday comparisons. Keep arithmetic consistent after rounding. Preserve management's qualifiers and distinguish its guidance from analyst estimates.

Attribute material claims with readable, accessible sources. Do not expose opaque internal IDs as if they were user-facing citations; retain those IDs in the evidence records. A clean layout can use linked footnotes or a compact source section, but must not erase provenance. Do not invent proprietary sources or broker counts to make a note look complete.

For model updates, decompose the important revisions and consensus gap. For portfolio updates, separate market movement, changed thesis, position changes, fees/funding and data-health failures. Never imply that no alert means a healthy monitor when activation/health is unknown.

Keep a brief proportionate to the decision. Long reports, 20 pages, 17 tables, fixed fonts or a minimum number of charts are not quality gates unless the user requests that deliverable. Required report render/verification workflows apply when actually producing Word/PDF/spreadsheet artifacts; do not claim a visual check from text inspection.

Preserve user-authored final wording as requested and separately flag factual disagreements; do not silently alter quoted final text. Save an audit copy when requested or required by the research workflow, with draft/final labels and references. Sharing, emailing or posting requires the host's existing authorisation. A drafted brief does not activate a watch or approve a trade.
