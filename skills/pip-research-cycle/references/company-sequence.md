# Model-first company research sequence

The user asks for the strongest logical arrangement of the supplied skills, with the model established before valuation and conclusion. This is the primary analyst sequence; portfolio implementation is a downstream extension.

Management-questions is a side branch during model building, audit and thesis validation. Rank questions by model/decision sensitivity. A new essential answer returns to the relevant assumption and invalidates dependent outputs. Do not wait for management when public evidence already answers the question or when independent work can continue.

## Why these dependencies

Actuals define the starting point. Operating data and guidance explain the drivers. Forecasts combine them into an auditable economic model. The audit catches stale consensus, unrealistic implied quarters, cost/cash/share assumptions and broken links before those errors reach valuation.

Valuation then asks what the price implies and whether the model justifies it. That produces a testable provisional thesis, which financial/operating evidence and a counter-case can confirm, weaken or reject. The investment conclusion synthesises those results; it does not appear as a verdict before modelling.

The raw financial-report skill contributes to two scoped passes: **model audit** verifies the input/model foundations; **thesis validation** tests the specific expectations after valuation. This retains its useful “兑现还是证伪” question without allowing unreconciled figures into valuation or forcing two complete financial reports.

## Handoff gates

- **Model-ready:** actuals, driver data and guidance availability have been reconciled or explicitly qualified. Important missing inputs are visible; undisclosed guidance is not invented.
- **Valuation-ready:** a versioned forecast exists with assumptions, scenarios, numerical/cash checks, comparable estimate differences and resolved or explicitly blocking material issues.
- **Conclusion-ready:** valuation scenarios, targeted thesis validation and the strongest counter-case are available. Any material forecast change has been propagated through the valuation and quoted outputs.

These are analytical gates. An unsized research view or no-action conclusion is valid; source gaps can limit confidence rather than force a fabricated result. Independent portfolio/risk and approval services still govern any later position proposal.

## Optional downstream extension

Investment conclusion -> exact instrument/strategy design -> net-cost and adverse-execution testing -> implementation challenge -> portfolio sizing/constraints -> approved paper proposal where supported -> monitoring/review.

An equity conclusion need not become a derivatives trade. An attractive underlying can have an unsuitable or costly available implementation. Monitoring feeds changed facts back to the affected model, valuation or conclusion; it does not silently broaden the mandate.
